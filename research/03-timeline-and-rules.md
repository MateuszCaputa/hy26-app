## Submission (default, all Proidea tasks)
- Project title, team name, members (1–6), description.
- **PDF presentation, max 10 slides.**
- Optional: snapshots, repo, demo links, graphics.
- Platform: the rules mention both "HackTribe" and "Challenge Rocket". Confirm on site.
- Partner-specific extras:
  - Huawei: `.hap`, public repo, video, AI_WORKFLOW.md.
  - Superteam: video of 3 minutes or less, live demo.
  - Kraków: video of 3 minutes or less, business model.
  - HubMI: demo link, UX mockups, maintenance cost estimate.

## Judging flow
1. **Phase 1:** a commission of 3 or more mentors scores the submissions on the platform. **You need at least 50% of the points here, or no prize.**
2. **Phase 2:** finalists pitch live to the jury.
   - The pitch is a separate scoring moment, so stage presence matters.
   - Jury decisions are final.
   - The jury may decline to award a prize.

## Default criteria (open tasks; partners override)
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Idea & Innovation | 30% |
| Relation to Category | 20% |
| Practical Applicability / Usability | 20% |
| **Design (visual)** | **20%** |
| Completeness & Implementation | 10% |

## AI-use policy (2026, in every task's details)
- AI tools are **allowed** and need **no prompt history**. Projects are **not scored on lines of code written by hand**.
- Significant AI use, models, APIs and datasets must be **disclosed**.
- **The team must explain and defend every technical decision.** "Functionality the team can't reasonably explain may hurt the technical score."
  - So every person needs to understand the architecture before the pitch. Budget 1h for a Claude-led "explain it to us" session.
- **Separate pre-existing code from hackathon work.** You may not present a pre-built product as hackathon work.
- Huawei additionally *requires* AI_WORKFLOW.md and calls agentic tools "strongly encouraged", which favours a Claude-heavy team.

## IP clauses worth knowing
- **Open tasks / Goldman:** copyright is **not** transferred.
- **HubMI (ROPS):** winning **requires signing a copyright transfer** agreement with Proidea.
- **Huawei:** non-exclusive, royalty-free licence to Huawei on accepting a prize.
- **Prize tax:** prizes are "including tax", about 10% PIT withheld, then split equally among members.
