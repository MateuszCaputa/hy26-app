## 2025 (4–5 Oct, about 2–3k people, 400+ projects, 141k PLN total)
**Open tasks, 5k each, one winner:**
- **Biohacking:** Rest & Blink. Eye-exercise app that uses the camera to verify you actually look away, with gamification and a freemium model. *This is the "screen app" idea, and it already won.*
- **Defence:** FOKZ Nav.
  - Navigation without GPS: an Android phone's motion sensors feed RoNIN (an AI model that estimates where you walked).
  - Integrated with TAK, a NATO-standard tactical map.
  - Four public repos and a live demo inside Tauron Arena.
- **Travel:** BeeSafeGirl. Women's safety app (SOS, map of dangerous places, chat, PL/UA/EN), working MVP.
- **Gamedev:** Hexateam, "Exorcist Hotline" (co-op horror). They won 2024 too. Their reasons:
  - finished 2h early
  - presentation themed to match the game
  - promo video
  - jury score 4.5/5

**Partner tasks:**
- **UKNF #Prompt2Code2 (35k):**
  1. *0 skillaw*: an AI-agent pipeline (spec, then about 50 GitHub issues, then reviewer agents, then code).
  2. Chlebki&Friends with TeniusAI.
  3. justCheckingHow: CI/CD plus a report on how they used the AI agents.
- **ZUS Retirement Simulator (25k):** NO SIGNAL "Emerytalna Przyszłość". 3rd place was praised for correct methodology and an engaging UI.
- **ZUS LifeRun (25k):** "Life Happend", a life-simulation game. The winners said they deliberately picked "the most abstract topic".
- **Małopolska Journey Radar (20k):** KN Solvro "JakDoczłapię", which combines delay data from all carriers. The distinction went to a team that said "every feature we proposed was implemented".
- **Kraków Volunteer Centre (5k, Polish):** DoBro. Full role-based access control (RBAC), GDPR, WCAG, three languages.
- **uczmnie.pl ProcessBot (5k):** MIKO1.

## Patterns that win (apply them)
1. **Partner tasks with a concrete brief beat open tasks on ROI.**
   - Open tasks have one winner from about 30–50 teams.
   - Partner tasks often pay three places, and specific requirements (Polish-only, a fixed stack, an English spec) shrink the field.
2. **The repeat winning shape:** a working web tool for the partner's own workflow, with **AI inside**, a **polished UI**, a live demo, a public repo and a roadmap or business slide.
3. **Fit to the brief beats novelty.** Teams who implemented *every* requested feature got distinctions.
4. **Finish early.** Winners had 2h for the pitch, video and slides. The pitch is a separate scoring phase.
5. **Repeat winners** (justCheckingHow, KN Solvro, HexaTeam, Deviniti, sth_fishy) show that process and polish carry over from year to year. That's a structural advantage, and a Claude workflow can replicate it.
6. **Show the AI process** when the task cares about it: UKNF rewarded the agent pipeline and its report. Huawei 2026 *requires* it.
7. **Crowding:** gamedev about 50 teams (the most crowded), CTF 31. The average is about 30–40 teams per task. No official per-task counts were found.

## 2026 vs prior years
- **No ministry tasks this year** (no Ministry of Finance, ZUS or UKNF), so the 35–60k pools are gone.
- The top pools are now Huawei 25k, Goldman 15k and ROPS 15k.
- Open tasks went up from 5k to 8k but still pay one winner each.
