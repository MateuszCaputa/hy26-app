# X1: REENTRY CTF

**Prize:** 5,000 PLN plus 5 annual IDA Pro Expert-4 licences from Hex-Rays (worth a lot, several thousand USD each).
**Registration:** separate Google Form (link in `sources/tasks-2026.json`). Join solo or as a team.

**Format:** a story-driven capture-the-flag competition (space station, rogue onboard AI). Categories:
- web
- cryptography
- reverse engineering
- pwn (binary exploitation)
- forensics
- **AI challenges**
- **real hardware**

There are beginner paths, and an on-site team game.

## Claude-fit
- Claude helps with crypto, web and reversing scripts. AI-challenges (prompt-injection style) suit it well.
- But a CTF is a time-competitive skill game, and the 2024 field was 31 teams of experienced players.
- It also competes for the same hours as the main build.

## Verdict
**Optional side quest** for one team member during dead time (e.g. while builds run). Don't make it the main bet.
