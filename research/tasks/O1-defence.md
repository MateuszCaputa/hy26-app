# O1: Defence (open task)

**Pool:** 8,000 PLN, one winner. Common rules: see `O0-open-tasks-common.md`. Source: `sources/txt/DEFENCE_details.txt`.

## Brief
Strengthen security and resilience. Detect threats earlier, reduce their impact, keep operations running. Areas: cyber, infrastructure resilience, **countering disinformation**, crisis response.

**Suggested directions:**
- a security-posture triage for small organisations
- **verifying suspicious messages, content or sources**
- emergency coordination and information sharing
- **mapping service dependencies and single points of failure**
- making security procedures easier to follow

"Consider what happens when information is incomplete, resources are limited, or services become unavailable."

## Past winners here
- 2025 FOKZ Nav: navigation without GPS using phone sensors + AI, integrated with the NATO TAK tactical map, live demo in the arena.
- 2024 DenisDefend: anti-phishing.
- 2023: a Raspberry Pi hardware password manager.

Judges like **real-world, operational, demonstrable** projects.

## Claude-fit ideas
- **Disinformation verifier:** paste or forward a message (SMS, Telegram post, screenshot), get a claim breakdown, source checks, known-narrative matching and a trust score. Polish context (Russian info-ops). Very demo-able. Claude does the multimodal and RAG parts well.
- **Offline-first crisis comms:** a PWA that works without internet (service worker, mesh through WebRTC/Bluetooth) for shelters during blackouts. Ties to 2025–26 drone incidents and power-grid concerns. Stronger story; more technical risk.
- **Dependency mapper for municipalities:** upload an asset list, get a graph of single points of failure and a "what if the power or ISP goes down" simulation.
