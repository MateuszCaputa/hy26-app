# X2: Prelint Challenge

**Prize:** USD 5,000 in **Prelint platform credits** (not cash).

**Ask:** build your HackYeah project *using Prelint* and show how it helped you move fast without losing control. Prelint does three things:
- tracks product and architecture decisions
- reviews pull requests for drift away from those decisions
- gives AI agents access to the decision history

## Claude-fit
- Stackable on any main project.
- It fits a Claude-agent workflow naturally: decisions recorded, Claude agents reading them, PR drift review.
- Our grill log (`99-grill-log.md`) is literally a decision history.

## Cost
Setup time (repo integration plus a learning curve). It's worthwhile only if under 30 minutes.

## Verdict
Do it if setup is quick. The credits have limited cash value, but they cost little extra effort.
