# O5: ImpactHer, Technology for Real Change (open task)

**Pool:** 8,000 PLN, one winner. Common rules: see `O0-open-tasks-common.md`. Source: `sources/txt/ImpactHer_TECHNOLOGY_FOR_REAL__details.txt`.

## Brief
A tech solution for a real challenge that particularly affects women. Areas: safety, health, education, professional development, access to services, the digital world. Start from a **well-identified need**; show practical potential and measurable change.

## Past winners nearby
BeeSafeGirl (2025 Travel): a women's walking-safety app with SOS and a danger map. Judges liked the clear human story and the working MVP.

## Claude-fit ideas
- **Pay-gap negotiation coach:** paste a job offer, get salary benchmarks for Poland plus role-play negotiation practice with AI. Measurable outcome.
- **Return-to-work after maternity:** a skills-refresh plan, a CV gap explainer, flexible-job matching.
- **Women's health navigator:** the Polish NFZ (public health fund) screening calendar (cytology, mammography) with reminders and booking links. Endometriosis and PCOS symptom tracking for doctor visits.
- **Online-harassment evidence kit:** collect screenshots, timestamp and hash them, generate a police-report PDF in Polish legal format.

## Competition guess
Probably a smaller field than AI or Defence, with a mixed-gender jury focus. **Strongest when the team includes women who present the problem first-hand.**

## Verdict
The best odds among the open tasks, but still one winner. A viable plan B if partner tasks feel wrong.
