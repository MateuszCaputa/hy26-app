# Open tasks: common facts (O1–O5)

- **Sponsor:** Proidea, the HackYeah organiser.
- **Prize:** 8,000 PLN each, **one winner**.
- **Language:** PL or EN.
- **IP:** stays with the team.

## Scoring (identical for all five)
| Criterion | Weight |
|---|---|
| Idea & Innovation | 30% |
| Relation to Category | 20% |
| Practical Applicability / Usability | 20% |
| **Design (visual)** | **20%** |
| Completeness & Implementation | 10% |

## Judging and submission
- **Two phases:**
  - Phase 1: platform review by 3 or more mentors. **You need at least 50% to stay eligible.**
  - Phase 2: live pitch to the jury.
- **Submission:** title, team, description, **PDF of 10 slides max**, plus an optional repo, demo and video.

## ROI reality
- Broad briefs mean the most teams (an estimated 30–50 each; AI is likely the largest), with a single winner.
- Historical winners here were polished, original **and** working, with an emotional or human story:
  - Rest & Blink (eye health)
  - BeeSafeGirl (women's safety)
  - FOKZ Nav (navigation without GPS)
  - DenisDefend (anti-phishing)
  - Straighty (posture)
- **Claude-fit is high** (no toolchain risk, design is 20%), but **the odds are low**. Ideation burns time because there's no spec.
- **The AI category warns of "unexpected twists / extra challenges"** appearing during the event, so its scope can move under you.
