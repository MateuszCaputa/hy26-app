# O4: Artificial Intelligence (open task)

**Pool:** 8,000 PLN, one winner. Common rules: see `O0-open-tasks-common.md`. Source: `sources/txt/ARTIFICIAL_INTELLIGENCE_details.txt`.

## Brief
A solution where **AI plays a meaningful role** in a specific user need.

**Suggested directions:**
- making complex information understandable
- adaptive learning
- accessibility
- creative assistance
- workflow automation
- decision support

**They want to see:**
- what role AI plays and how the components fit together
- the reasons behind technical decisions, plus capabilities and limits
- **how users can verify outputs and stay in control**
- a concrete use case

**Warning:** "don't be surprised if a few unexpected twists, themes, or extra challenges appear along the way." The brief may change mid-event.

## Past winners
- KN Solvro EasyTalk (2024, 20k, sponsored by the Ministry of Digital Affairs): an AI agent for navigating hard-to-use public websites.
- 2025 UKNF Prompt2Code winners: agent pipelines.

## Claude-fit
Perfect technically, but **this is the most crowded category** (every team has an AI idea), with one winner and a moving brief. The worst odds per hour despite the best fit.

## Verdict
Avoid as the primary target. If dual submission is allowed, our main project (which will have strong AI) could also be entered here at no cost.
