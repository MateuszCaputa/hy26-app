# O2: Sport & Healthcare (open task)

**Pool:** 8,000 PLN, one winner. Common rules: see `O0-open-tasks-common.md`. *The site's Details PDF is the same file as the Rules PDF (no extra detail published).*

## Brief
Health and activity data (smartwatches, fitness apps, lab results, treatment history, wellbeing) is scattered and hard to act on. Build something that **combines sport, physical health, mental wellbeing and access to care**, and **helps users make better decisions**, not just monitor.

## Past winners in this space
- Rest & Blink (2025 Biohacking): screen-break and eye exercises with camera verification.
- Straighty (2024 Wellness): a posture app.
- mDawka (2023 Health).

**The screen-break app idea has already won once.** Repeating it needs a strong new angle.

## Claude-fit ideas
- **Lab-results explainer + action plan:** upload a PDF of blood test results, get a plain-Polish explanation, trends against previous tests, "what to ask your doctor", and NFZ (public health fund) specialist booking links. Multimodal parsing plus a nice UI. Very Claude-friendly.
- **Unified health timeline:** Apple Health / Google Fit export plus lab PDFs plus a mood journal, with an AI coach that suggests one concrete action per day.
- **Screen-break 2.0:** combine eye strain, posture (webcam pose estimation through MediaPipe in the browser) and hydration into one "desk health" score with a team leaderboard. Fully in-browser, no install.

## Risks
Medical advice and liability questions from judges. Health data is sensitive (GDPR). Mention local processing.

## Verdict
Easy to build and good for design scores, but crowded with one winner. Low ROI unless the idea is exceptional.
