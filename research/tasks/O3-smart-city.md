# O3: Smart City (open task)

**Pool:** 8,000 PLN, one winner. Common rules: see `O0-open-tasks-common.md`. *The Details PDF is the same as the Rules PDF.*

## Brief
Cities are at capacity. Improve mobility, resources, energy, citizen communication, urban data, public services and crisis response. "Technology that helps cities work better."

## Past winners nearby
- KN Solvro "JakDoczłapię" (2025 Journey Radar, 20k): unified delays across all carriers.
- SmartBike (2024, safe cycling).
- WorkZone Finder (2023).

Judges like **real open data plus a working map**.

## Claude-fit ideas
- **Kraków accessibility router:** the P5 project reused here, if dual submission is allowed.
- **"Zgłoś problem" AI triage** (report-a-problem): a resident snaps a photo (pothole, broken lamp, illegal dumping); AI classifies it, geolocates it, deduplicates it against nearby reports and routes it to the right city unit. Includes a city dashboard.
- **Live public-transport disruption assistant** on Kraków's GTFS-RT open data (pulled into the app). Risk: live data feeds.

## Verdict
Low odds with one winner. Only worth it as a **second submission** of the P5 project.
